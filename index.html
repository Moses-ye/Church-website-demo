About Favour Church

Established in 2010, we are a Bible-based church committed to glorifying God through worship, discipleship, and mission.

Our Vision & Mission

Favour Church exists to glorify God by making disciples of Jesus Christ through the transforming power of the Holy Spirit.

We are committed to:

  • Biblical teaching and preaching
  • Authentic worship and prayer
  • Loving fellowship and community
  • Effective evangelism and outreach
  • Compassionate service to our community
